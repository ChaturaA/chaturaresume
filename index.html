Projects

Kundalini — A Journey Through Fashion & Consciousness

Jan 2025 – Feb 2025

Kundalini – A Journey Through Fashion and Consciousness Kundalini is a third-year fashion collection inspired by the awakening of the seven chakras, where clothing becomes a medium for spiritual and aesthetic expression. Each ensemble embodies the essence of a chakra through its colors, symbols, and mindful design—red for grounding at the Root, green for compassion at the Heart, violet for enlightenment at the Crown, and more. Crafted in sustainable Khadi cotton, the collection highlights crop tops adorned with hand-drawn chakra motifs, delicate beadwork, and energy-aligned semi-precious stones. Tie-dyed harem pants echo the fluid movement of prana, while Rudraksha tassels bring spiritual resonance. Accessories such as layered bead armlets and chokers enrich the Indo-fusion aesthetic, weaving tradition with contemporary silhouettes. Kundalini transforms fashion into an inner journey—where every outfit is not just worn, but deeply experienced, aligning body, mind, and spirit through design.

Satrangi Leheriyo — Riwayat 2024

Jan 2024 – Mar 2024

Satrangi Leheriyo – Waves of Tradition and Modernity Our second-year collection, Satrangi Leheriyo, draws inspiration from the timeless Rajasthani tie-dye technique, Leheriya—a craft that mirrors the rhythm of waves and celebrates the vibrant spirit of Rajasthan. Traditionally associated with festive occasions such as Teej and Rajasthani weddings, Leheriya carries a rich cultural legacy while embodying joy, prosperity, and celebration. The collection embraces a palette of festive hues—red for love, orange for prosperity, green for joy, blue for spirituality, and pink for celebration—infused into both hand-painted and digitally rendered Leheriya patterns. These ripple-like designs come alive on airy fabrics like georgette and chiffon, chosen for their lightness, fluidity, and graceful movement. Blending tradition with contemporary silhouettes, Satrangi Leheriyo introduces Indo-Western fusions: traditional drapes are reimagined with structured jackets, flowing capes, and tailored trousers, reflecting a dialogue between heritage and modern style. Accents of gold piping and subtle embellishments add a festive sheen, elevating the collection with understated elegance. Satrangi Leheriyo is not just clothing—it is a celebration of Rajasthan’s artistry, reinvented for today’s stage with youthful vibrancy and timeless charm.

Tropical Waves — Foundation Year Collection

Jan 2023 – Mar 2023

Tropical Waves – Foundation Year Collection “Tropical Waves” is a vibrant children’s wear collection developed during my foundation year in fashion design. Drawing inspiration from the lively spirit of tropical beaches and ocean waves, the collection captures the carefree energy of summer holidays—sunsets, sea breeze, and playful freedom. Comfort and movement were central to the design process, with lightweight, breathable fabrics and flowy silhouettes making the outfits ideal for beach outings and poolside fun. The color palette takes cues from glowing sunsets, turquoise waters, and coral reefs, while hand-rendered prints reflect the joy and rhythm of nature. The collection features playful garments such as tunics, wrap dresses, and easy playsuits, blending child-friendly practicality with a bold, cheerful aesthetic. Surface design became a key tool in giving each piece its own expressive identity while maintaining a cohesive theme. This show marked my first exploration into thematic storytelling in fashion—balancing creativity with function and setting the foundation for my journey as a designer. View project
